01 / The hours no one sees

01 / Dreams

How will you begin to dream something no machine can dream?

I’ll begin with the parts of life I can’t reduce to a prompt: the people I care about, the places that change me, and the uncertainty of building something of my own. A machine might imagine the same future, but it would never be taking my risk to get there.

02 / Memory

If memory is built from shared stories, which dreams do you want written into the past?

I want the late nights spent building something to sit beside the trips and ordinary moments with my friends. When I look back, I want to remember who I shared that life with, and not only what I managed to achieve.

03 / Identity

What remains of you when all your stories are rewritten?

Even if the story changes, I still have to choose how I treat people and what I do next. I think that is where I remain: in the choices I keep making, even when nobody is recording them.

04 / Agency

If you seek to realize your hopes through AI, how will you ensure that it remains faithful to your will?

I’ll keep asking whether what AI helps me create actually serves the life I want. That means setting the goal myself, questioning the suggestions, and being willing to reject an impressive result that feels wrong. I can use AI to build faster while taking responsibility for the direction.

The direction is still mine.
